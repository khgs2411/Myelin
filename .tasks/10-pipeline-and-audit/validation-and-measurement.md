# Validation And Measurement

## Outcome

Myelin can validate memory structure and measure answer quality without hiding failures.

## Why it matters

The archive repeatedly returns to validation, self-correction, and measurement because memory quality decays without feedback.

## Scope

- Structural validation.
- Semantic warnings.
- Measurement questions.
- Score records.
- Non-blocking maintenance items.

## Done means

- Validation failures stop write workflows or clearly degrade outputs.
- Measurement can produce actionable maintenance work.
- Warnings are deduplicated where possible.
